# Code Of Conduct

LexiChess is committed to making collaboration welcoming, safe, and constructive for contributors, users, and community members.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and patience
- Giving and receiving feedback respectfully
- Assuming good intent while still being honest
- Focusing criticism on ideas, code, and product decisions rather than people
- Helping other contributors learn and succeed

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation
- Personal attacks, insults, or hostile behavior
- Trolling, dogpiling, or intentionally derailing discussion
- Publishing private information without permission
- Repeated bad-faith behavior after a request to stop

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, project chats, and other spaces where someone is representing LexiChess.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that violate this code of conduct.

Maintainers may also take any action they consider appropriate, including temporary or permanent bans from project spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainers through the contact path listed in the repository profile or by opening a private maintainer communication channel when available.

## Maintainer Commitment

Maintainers are expected to enforce this policy consistently and fairly. They should explain moderation actions when it is safe and reasonable to do so.

This document is adapted for the needs of this repository and intentionally kept short so it stays readable and actionable.
